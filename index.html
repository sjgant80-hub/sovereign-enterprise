Run your own company

This browser tab becomes a device in your own company. Found it, invite teammates by link, and every device syncs peer to peer — same machine over tabs, other machines over a direct encrypted link. Nothing goes to a server. Setting up a whole office? Wire it, step by step.

If this laptop is stolen or lost — what to do, and what a thief gets
  1. Cut it off. From another device of the company, open People and revoke the lost device's grant — an admin revokes staff, the owner revokes an admin. From that moment every device refuses anything that key signs. What it wrote before stays, and nobody can rewrite it: each write names the one before, so a forged past is caught, with a proof, on every device.
  2. If it was the owner's device, it cannot be revoked — the owner is the root of the company. Bring the owner back on a new machine: open this page, choose Run your own → Restore the owner from recovery cards, and type any two of the three cards. Then connect the new machine to another device of the company (Connect another machine, below). The restored owner holds its writes until its own earlier writes have come back to it, so it never forks its own history.
  3. Before anything goes wrong: print the owner's recovery cards when you found the company and keep them in three different places; grant a second admin on another machine; turn on full-disk encryption (BitLocker or Device encryption on Windows, FileVault on a Mac) and a screen lock on every machine.

What a thief with the laptop gets. With full-disk encryption on and the laptop off or locked: nothing. Without it, someone who reads the browser's own files straight off the disk can recover the device key and the key that seals its stored records — so they could read this device's copy of the company and sign as this device until its grant is revoked (step 1). If it was taken unlocked with the browser open, they can use it as you could. If it was the owner's laptop and they got that far, assume they can sign as the owner: the company's other admins keep working, and the owner key cannot be removed.

What no thief gets. The key through a web page: the browser hands its bytes to no page, not even this one, so nothing running in a browser can copy it out. A changed past: every write names the one before, so nothing anyone wrote can be rewritten. The rest of the company: every other device keeps working.
