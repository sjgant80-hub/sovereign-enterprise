SOVEREIGN ENTERPRISE

A full AI company on its own devices. Six AI agents run sales, service, finance, operations, systems and audit end to end; two humans watch. Every device is a brain — verified pattern libraries, re-checked on that device's own data, sharing what they learn over a signed peer-to-peer spine. Money and security calls need a second, independent brain to agree. Nothing leaves the building. booting the company…

Kernel-backed: this page imports src/company · agent · brain · grow · screen · crdt · gate · canon · keys · node · vault · books · lease · sim — the same modules the test suite and the mutation witness prove in CI. Each device holds its own Ed25519 key and its own replica; the company is a pure function of the set of signed writes a device holds. The brains are the Konomi SLM's verified pattern libraries (Konomi architecture — Thomas Frumkin), grown by the seed-library ribosome (sjgant80-hub, credited in NOTICE). Sales runs on real data — the UCI Online Shoppers dataset (Sakar & Kastro, 2018, CC BY 4.0); the other seven streams are realistic-synthetic, each generated from a stated rule plus seeded noise (shown in Brains). The entropy-guided design lens (GEP, WIPER, BES, Dual-Map) is the work of Gary W. Floyd, Lumiea Systems Research Division (ThunderStruck Service, LLC). The independent second brain and the held-for-a-human verdict draw on his “From GEP Stabilization to Dual-Map Ethical Reasoning,” Derivation v0.2.
