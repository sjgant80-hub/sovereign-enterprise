The AI-native office

Five people and the machines they own run what fifty people run today. The AI does the work; AI-native leads oversee it; the company's data stays on its own machines, and the few calls that go out per use carry only what each one needs. Here is the whole set-up — the roles, the hardware at every step, how it hooks together, and what it costs — with every figure computed live from sourced UK data.

1 · The shift: from software that helps to AI that does the work

Office software rents a company its own logic, data and screens, one seat at a time, forever. The first wave of AI added another seat on top: an assistant on every desk. The people still do the work, so the bill only grows.

The AI-native office takes the other road. The work itself — answering the customer, qualifying the lead, matching the invoice, booking the van, onboarding the starter — is done by AI agents running on machines the company owns. A small number of AI-native people direct those agents, check what matters, and handle the judgement, the relationships and the exceptions.

4 · How it hooks together

Every machine runs the same signed, gated company spine. The company is a pure function of the signed writes a machine holds — so every device, every agent and every person sees the same company, whether the network is up, down or split.

Signed by the device that made it

Every write — a task, a message, a deal, a ledger entry — carries the Ed25519 signature of the person's or agent's own device. Nothing anonymous enters the company.

Checked before it is read

Every incoming write passes the gate on its raw bytes: size, encoding, exact grammar, canonical form, replay, signature. Forged, altered, replayed or malformed writes never reach the company.

One company on every device

Concurrent edits merge the same way everywhere; a device that was offline catches up when it reconnects. Partitions, delays and duplicates cannot make two devices disagree.

Authority is a tree

The owner's key makes leads; leads grant each person and agent exactly the parts of the company they work in. An agent cannot touch the books unless it was granted the books.

The books prove themselves

Every posted entry is sequenced into an append-only transparency log. Anyone — an auditor, a bank — can verify an entry is in the books and that nothing was ever rewritten.

Personal data, sealed per person

Personal details are stored only under a key for that person. Erasing a person destroys the key: every copy goes dark at once, and the history still verifies.

A leaver's device locks itself

Each device's local data is held on a lease it renews while its grant is live. Revoke the grant and the lease runs out; the cache on that device seals itself.

Nothing leaves the building

The company's data lives on its own devices and its own AI boxes. Specialist services receive only what a single call needs — the voice of one call, the text of one hard question.

5 · The full AI company: every device is a brain

Go one step further than an office with AI in it. In the full AI company the agents run every department end to end and the people watch. Each agent is a device on the spine with its own key, a grant scoped to its department, and a brain: a small, readable library of verified patterns (the Konomi SLM) — each pattern earned its place by predicting cases it never saw, and it answers only inside what it was verified on. Outside that, it abstains. It never guesses.

The brains are not one big model in one place. They are distributed — a mycelium — and these rules make them one intelligence:

Trust nothing until it re-verifies here

An agent loads even its own department's brain file only after it re-checks it on its own data. A brain offered by another device is adopted the same way — so a poisoned brain dies at the first device that checks it, whatever it claims about itself.

Ask the mesh

Work arrives at a door that does not know who handles it. An agent that cannot answer posts a signed question on the spine; the device whose brain covers the case posts a signed reply, with a receipt that names the pattern and the score. There is no shared memory: every answer the company acts on is a record every device holds.

Learn what you are asked

An agent asked about the same kind of work again and again takes the published brain for it — and keeps it only if it re-verifies on that agent's own data. The company's knowledge spreads to where the work lands.

A second, independent brain for money and security

Holding a payment, setting credit terms, quarantining a machine: a second brain on another device, grown on its own data, must agree. A copy of the same brain is not a check and is refused. Disagreement goes to a person.

Regrow on outcomes — and only promote a winner

Outcomes come back. An agent regrows its brain on them, and the new brain replaces the old only if it beats it on outcomes neither brain grew on.

Screen everything before it is read

Customer text is checked for invisible characters, direction tricks, smuggled hidden text and injection phrasing before any brain sees it. Anything flagged is quarantined unread. A new kind of attack a person confirms becomes a signed antibody; every device re-checks it on its own genuine messages and adopts it only if it fires on none — caught once, refused everywhere.

Watch how every agent behaves

A valid key and grant are not enough. Each agent's own habits are learned and frozen; an agent doing one thing every time, keeping clockwork time, or acting off its own work is paused by the behaviour watch (automatic) until a person clears it — after Gary W. Floyd's Behavioral Entropy Signatures (Lumiea Systems Research Division — ThunderStruck Service, LLC).

A language model only where no brain reaches

Free text — reading what a customer wrote, sending it to the right desk, drafting a reply — goes to a model the company chooses: on its own machine, in the browser, or a provider with its own key. The spend is capped before any paid call, every call is audited on the spine, a model never acts alone on money or security, and its replies are drafts a person sends.

This is not a mock-up. The same code the live page runs, re-run headlessly from a fixed seed — the door, the agents, the mesh, the books — gives these results on every machine, byte for byte. Re-run it: node tools/run-company.mjs --check.

What those numbers prove — and what they don’t

A job where the answer is nearly always “no” makes “right” look good even for doing nothing. So each job is set against doing nothing on the same calls, with an exact test, and the second brain is judged by what it actually caught.

  2. AI boxThe finance agent matches it to the order and the delivery note, codes it, and drafts the journal.

7 · What it costs: five years, three ways

The move, one-off — and when it pays back

Going AI-native is a change for people, not only a purchase. The model counts the cost of the move itself: statutory redundancy for the roles that go, the old team working alongside the agents while they prove themselves, notice and consultation, training for the people who stay, and a contingency.

If the agents do less of the work

Everything above assumes the agents do 90% of the operational work end to end. This is the same case at other shares — the number a board should test first.

10 · The AI-native employee

The people who remain are not doing the old jobs faster. They direct a workforce of agents, and they decide what the agents cannot. Three rules make that work, and the live company runs all three:

An agent answers only what it can verify

Each agent's brain answers from a rule that proved itself on cases it never learned from, or it abstains. An answer it cannot verify is asked of another device's brain or put on the Watch list for a person — never guessed.

A second, independent brain confirms what matters

Money and security calls need a different brain on another device to agree; a copy of the same brain agreeing proves nothing and is refused. When no brain can confirm a call, it waits for a person, who chooses what happens.

A model drafts; it never sends

A language model may route a message or draft a reply, and every use is recorded on the company's record. It never acts alone on money or security, and its replies are held as drafts until a person sends them.
